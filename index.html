Contributor Highlight.
Meet Liz Mallers.
We're thrilled to spotlight Liz Mallers- Certified Sexuality Educator, one of our top contributors on Bariatric Surgery Corner.

Liz is a certified sexuality educator who works with couples and individuals to enhance their intimate pleasures. Through her psychoeducational work, Liz helps her clients unlearn the unwanted internalized messages about sexuality that create barriers to deep intimate connection, and rebuilds their understanding of sex and intimacy for a healthier and satisfying sex life. She specializes in helping post-op bariatric patients take on the challenges of navigating their intimate lives while undergoing the massive mental and physical changes that come with bariatric surgery.
